shackey

Flash the latest firmware to your shackey (ESP32-C3) directly from the browser.

Your browser does not support Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. Web Serial needs a secure connection (https).

How to

  1. short the boot pin and connect the shackey via USB
  2. click Connect and choose USB JTAG/serial debug unit
  3. click Install, wait until it's done
  4. remove the boot short and reset the device

Your uploaded keys are kept, only bootloader, partition table and app are written.
